Before you decide

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Understand the fit before planning a change.

Bring your questions
What should I expect from a demo?

We walk through your actual properties, contracts and workflows, not a generic script. You'll see how Tenants10 handles your real use case, including what your tenants or occupants would see in the app.

Do I need to replace my current systems?

No. Tenants10 is built to be your system of record for properties, contracts, tenants and maintenance, and it's designed to take on data from whatever you're using today, including spreadsheets.

Can I bring my spreadsheets?

Yes. Spreadsheet import is built into the platform. We'll review your data together and help you decide what comes in first.

What does implementation involve, and what does it cost?

Pricing depends on your setup: residential agencies are priced per unit, facility management businesses per building, plus any add-on modules. Most agencies are live within a few weeks of data review. We'll scope rollout, data import and support together and send a proposal.

Is the AI shown here available?

AI-powered contract reading is live in the platform today. The direct Q&A assistant shown above is in beta; ask us for a current look at both in your demo. Either way, AI output is always a draft: nothing is written to a building, unit, tenant or contract until a staff member reviews and confirms it.

What happens to the information I enter here?

Submitting this form sends your details directly to our team by email — nothing is stored in a database. We'll use it only to follow up about your demo, and we never share your details.

Is my data secure once we're live?

Yes. Production data is hosted in Switzerland, every staff member's access is controlled by role-based permissions you configure, and we never share tenant data with third parties.

What if we want to stop using Tenants10?

Your data is yours. We'll help you export it if you ever decide to leave, with no lock-in contracts.
