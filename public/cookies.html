Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Tenants10 KlG ("Tenants10", "we") uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) on tenants10.com and in the Tenants10 web application, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. "Similar technologies" includes browser local storage and session storage, which our web application uses in the same way as a cookie for the purposes of this Policy.

2. Categories of cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary (always active) — keep you logged in, remember your session, protect against cross-site request forgery, and remember basic preferences such as your selected language. The Service cannot function without these, so they cannot be switched off.
  • Functional — remember non-essential preferences (such as a collapsed sidebar or a chosen view) to make the Service more convenient to use.
  • Analytics (website only, where enabled) — help us understand how visitors use tenants10.com, in aggregate, so we can improve it. These are only set with your consent on the public website.

We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Your choices

When you first visit tenants10.com, you can choose which non-essential cookie categories to allow via the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing the "tenants10-cookie-consent" entry in your browser's site data, which will show the banner again, or by adjusting your browser's own cookie settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent the website and app from working correctly, including staying logged in.

The Tenants10 web application (workspace.tenants10.com) only sets the strictly necessary cookies/local storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings — no analytics or advertising cookies are set there.

4. Contact

Questions about this Cookie Policy? Contact support@tenants10.com.
